## 🌟 1. Financial & Commitments Hub

### 1. 💳 Smart Expense & Subscription Tracker (`/expenses`)
- **What it does:** Tracks recurring expenses (WiFi bill, phone recharge, rent, Spotify/Netflix, gym fees, college tuition).
- **AI Automation:** Srushti calculates days remaining until due date, notifies you 3 days before, and alerts you if recurring bills collide with your low-budget weeks.
- **Key Features:** Monthly recurring cost breakdown, countdown badges (`Due in 2 days`), payment mark-as-paid, and 1-tap bill creation via chat.

### 2. 📄 Document Vault & Expiry Tracker (`/documents`)
- **What it does:** Keeps a secure register of important personal documents (Passport, Driving License, University ID, Insurance, Certificates, Lease Agreement).
- **AI Automation:** Tracks expiration and renewal deadlines, alerting you 30 days and 7 days prior so you never get caught with expired documents.
- **Key Features:** Document category tags, renewal checklist, notes for document numbers/locations, and status badges (`Valid`, `Expiring Soon`, `Expired`).

## 🎓 2. Academics, Skills & Career Command

### 3. 📚 Syllabus & Exam Preparation Command Center (`/exams`)
- **What it does:** Dedicated hub for college courses, subjects, chapters, and upcoming exam dates.
- **AI Automation:** Tell Srushti *"I have CAO exam on 25th with 5 chapters"*, and she splits syllabus chapters across your free calendar blocks with buffer revision slots.
- **Key Features:** Subject syllabus checklist, exam countdown timers, preparation completion percentage, and revision schedule view.

### 4. 📖 Reading & Course Progress Tracker (`/learning`)
- **What it does:** Tracks books you are reading, online courses enrolled in (Coursera, Udemy, YouTube playlists), and technical skills you are learning.
- **AI Automation:** Blocks out daily 20–30 minute reading or study sprints into your schedule and tracks pages/lectures completed.
- **Key Features:** Reading speed calculation, pages logged today, chapter bookmarks, and completion certificates record.

## 🏃 3. Health, Wellness & Daily Energy

### 5. 💊 Medication & Health Routine Companion (`/health`)
- **What it does:** Daily tracker for medications, vitamins, water intake, and workout routines.
- **AI Automation:** Timely alerts linked to breakfast/dinner times (*"Time for Vitamin D after lunch"*), daily hydration targets, and workout streak logging.
- **Key Features:** 1-tap pill check-off, visual 8-glass water meter, workout split manager (Push/Pull/Legs), and sleep schedule tracking.

### 6. 🧠 Mood & Energy Reflection Journal (`/journal`)
- **What it does:** Fast 15-second daily reflection log: Mood score (1–5), energy level, and 1 sentence of gratitude or highlight.
- **AI Automation:** Detects correlation between sleep/workload and mood (*"You report higher energy on days you exercise in the morning"*).
- **Key Features:** Daily mood emoji selector, gratitude scratchpad, weekly sentiment analytics, and AI encouraging feedback.

## 👥 4. Social, Relationships & Memory

### 7. 🎂 Relationship & Important Dates Vault (`/people`)
- **What it does:** Stores key details about family, friends, mentors, and colleagues (Birthdays, Anniversaries, gift ideas, promises made, last contacted date).
- **AI Automation:** Srushti reminds you 2 days before birthdays with gift/greeting ideas and flags when you haven't caught up with a close friend in 30+ days.
- **Key Features:** Contact birthday calendar, promise log (*"Lent book to Rahul"*), conversation notes, and relationship tags.

### 8. 🛫 Travel & Trip Planner Companion (`/trips`)
- **What it does:** Organizes upcoming trips, flights, train tickets, packing checklists, and daily travel itineraries.
- **AI Automation:** Converts ticket confirmation text into structured schedule blocks and generates customized packing lists based on destination weather.
- **Key Features:** Interactive packing checklist, flight/train departure countdown, day-by-day itinerary timeline, and hotel details card.

## 🏠 5. Personal Assets & Productivity

### 9. ⚡ Quick Capture "Brain Dump" Inbox (`/inbox`)
- **What it does:** A zero-friction scratchpad to quickly type or voice-record random thoughts, links, and ideas.
- **AI Automation:** Srushti's background agent reads your inbox and automatically converts items into tasks, calendar events, memories, or expenses.
- **Key Features:** Voice capture button, 1-tap "AI Auto-Sort", quick tags, and instant conversion chips.

### 10. 🔧 Home, Vehicle & Appliance Warranty Manager (`/assets`)
- **What it does:** Logs vehicle service dates (bike/car maintenance, oil change, insurance), home rent renewal, and electronics warranties.
- **AI Automation:** Sends reminder alerts when vehicle servicing is due or when an electronics warranty is about to lapse.
- **Key Features:** Service interval tracker (e.g. *Every 6 months or 5,000 km*), warranty receipt photos, and customer care emergency numbers.

### 11. 🎯 Lifelong Bucket List & Dream Milestones (`/bucketlist`)
- **What it does:** Visual dream board for lifelong ambitions (places to visit, adventures, creative projects, financial milestones).
- **AI Automation:** Connects bucket list dreams to actionable annual goals and suggests free weekend slots to make progress.
- **Key Features:** Visual picture cards, target target year, inspiration notes, and "Achieved" celebration trophies.

### 12. 🆘 Emergency Life Card & ICE Contacts (`/emergency`)
- **What it does:** Instant 1-tap accessible emergency health card with blood group, allergies, emergency contacts (ICE), and medical notes.
- **AI Automation:** Accessible offline directly from the profile without complex navigation in urgent situations.
- **Key Features:** Large emergency call buttons, medical ID summary, and doctor contacts.
